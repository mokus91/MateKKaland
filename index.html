Trust & safety

Non-negotiable standards

XHUB is planned for lawful adult content only. No minors, coercion, non-consensual intimate imagery, trafficking, hidden-camera material, impersonation or copyright infringement. Every participant must be an adult and must have given informed, documented consent. Reports must be reviewed promptly, with a clear removal and appeal process.

Before public launch, the operator must implement robust age assurance, KYC/consent records, privacy-by-design, content moderation, copyright notices, data retention controls, local legal review and a payment provider that explicitly accepts the business category.
